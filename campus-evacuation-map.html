DURING AN EMERGENCY

Follow The Safe Route.

Remember these important steps when evacuating the campus.

1

STAY CALM

Stay calm and listen carefully to instructions from teachers and authorized school personnel.

2

USE SAFE EXITS

Use the nearest safe emergency exit and follow the designated evacuation route.

3

FOLLOW THE ROUTE

Stay on the marked evacuation path and avoid unsafe or restricted areas.

4

GO TO ASSEMBLY AREA

Proceed to the designated assembly area and stay with your class or group.

Emergency Exits

Know the nearest safe exit from your classroom, office, or current location.

  • Identify exits before an emergency happens.
  • Keep exit paths clear.
  • Use the nearest safe exit when instructed.

Evacuation Routes

Follow the designated route instead of taking shortcuts through unsafe areas.

  • Follow posted evacuation signs.
  • Stay with your class or group.
  • Follow instructions from school personnel.

Assembly Areas

Proceed to the designated assembly area after leaving the building.

  • Stay with your class or assigned group.
  • Do not return to the building without permission.
  • Wait for further instructions.
