TSUNAMI SAFETY GUIDE

Move Fast.
Get to Higher Ground.

A tsunami can arrive quickly after an undersea earthquake or other disturbance. Knowing the warning signs and moving to a safe location immediately can help protect you and others.

PREPARE

Three Things To Remember

Remember these important actions when there is a tsunami threat.

01

RECOGNIZE

Recognize natural warning signs such as a strong or long earthquake, unusual sea level changes, or a loud ocean roar.

02

MOVE

Move immediately to higher ground or farther inland when a tsunami warning or natural warning sign occurs.

03

STAY INFORMED

Listen to official tsunami warnings and follow evacuation instructions from authorities and school personnel.

SAFETY TIMELINE

Before, During & After

Follow these safety steps when there is a tsunami threat.

BEFORE

Know Your Evacuation Plan

  • Learn whether your area is near the coast or a tsunami hazard zone.
  • Know the designated evacuation routes and safe assembly areas.
  • Identify higher ground or safe inland locations that can be reached quickly.
  • Prepare an emergency Go Bag with water, food, medicines, and important supplies.
  • Learn and remember the natural warning signs of a tsunami.
DURING

Move Immediately

  • If you feel a strong or long earthquake near the coast, move to higher ground as soon as it is safe to do so.
  • If the sea suddenly pulls back or rises unusually, move away from the coast.
  • Follow official evacuation orders and move inland or to higher ground.
  • Do not go to the shore to watch the waves or check what is happening.
  • Stay away from coastal and low-lying areas until authorities give the all-clear.
AFTER

Stay Away Until Safe

  • Stay in a safe location until authorities announce that it is safe to return.
  • Remember that a tsunami can consist of multiple waves.
  • Avoid floodwater, damaged buildings, debris, and downed electrical lines.
  • Check yourself and others for injuries and request emergency assistance when necessary.
  • Follow school and local authority instructions before returning to affected areas.
CCTC REMINDER

Evacuate Immediately When Instructed

During a tsunami threat, remain calm and follow the instructions of teachers, school personnel, and emergency responders. Move to the designated safe area and do not return until authorities announce that it is safe.
