The CCTC Disaster Ready Portal is a school disaster preparedness website designed to help students, teachers, staff, and the school community learn how to prepare for and respond to different emergencies and disasters.

ABOUT THE PORTAL

What is CCTC Disaster Ready?

CCTC Disaster Ready is a disaster preparedness portal created for the Consolatrix College of Toledo City community. It provides easy-to-understand information and useful resources that can help people prepare before, stay safe during, and recover after emergencies.

The portal brings important disaster preparedness information together in one place so that members of the CCTC community can quickly access safety guides, emergency checklists, evacuation information, and other helpful resources.

OUR PURPOSE

Building a Prepared School Community

The main purpose of the CCTC Disaster Ready Portal is to promote disaster awareness and preparedness within the school community.

By providing simple instructions and accessible emergency resources, the portal encourages students, teachers, and staff to become more prepared and confident when responding to emergencies.
