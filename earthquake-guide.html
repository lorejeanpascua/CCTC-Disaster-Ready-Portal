EARTHQUAKE SAFETY GUIDE

Stay Calm.
Know What To Do.

Earthquakes can happen without warning. Being prepared and knowing the correct safety procedures can help protect you, your family, and the CCTC community.

DURING AN EARTHQUAKE

Drop. Cover. Hold On.

Remember these three important steps when an earthquake occurs.

1

DROP

Drop down to your hands and knees immediately. This position prevents you from being knocked over.

2

COVER

Take cover under a sturdy table or desk. Protect your head and neck with your arms.

3

HOLD ON

Hold on to your shelter until the shaking stops. Stay in position and remain calm.

Before an Earthquake

Preparation is important before an earthquake happens.

  • Prepare an emergency Go Bag.
  • Identify safe places inside classrooms and buildings.
  • Know the evacuation routes and assembly areas.
  • Secure heavy furniture and objects.
  • Practice earthquake drills regularly.

During an Earthquake

Stay calm and focus on protecting yourself.

  • Drop, Cover, and Hold On.
  • Stay away from windows and glass.
  • Do not run outside while shaking continues.
  • Do not use elevators.
  • If outdoors, move to an open area.

After an Earthquake

Remain alert because aftershocks may occur.

  • Check yourself and others for injuries.
  • Follow evacuation instructions.
  • Proceed calmly to the designated assembly area.
  • Stay away from damaged buildings.
  • Wait for official announcements before returning.
CCTC COMMUNITY REMINDER

Follow Your School's Evacuation Procedures

During emergencies, always listen to teachers, school personnel, and designated emergency responders. Proceed calmly to the assigned evacuation or assembly area.
