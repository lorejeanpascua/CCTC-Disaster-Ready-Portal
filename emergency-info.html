DISASTER SAFETY GUIDE

Emergency Information

Learn what to do before, during, and after a disaster. Understanding basic safety procedures can help protect you, your family, and the CCTC community.

BE INFORMED

Know What to Do in an Emergency

Emergencies can happen unexpectedly. Stay calm, prepare ahead of time, and follow instructions from authorized emergency personnel.

Before a Disaster

  • Prepare an emergency go-bag.
  • Know your evacuation routes and assembly areas.
  • Save important emergency contact numbers.
  • Secure heavy furniture and hazardous materials.
  • Monitor official weather and disaster advisories.

During a Disaster

  • Stay calm and assess your immediate surroundings.
  • Follow official warnings and safety instructions.
  • Move away from dangerous areas when instructed.
  • Assist others only when it is safe to do so.
  • Avoid spreading unverified information.

After a Disaster

  • Check yourself and others for injuries.
  • Avoid damaged buildings and fallen power lines.
  • Use phones for essential communications.
  • Wait for official clearance before returning to unsafe areas.
  • Follow instructions from emergency responders.
EARTHQUAKE

Earthquake Safety Guidelines

Earthquakes can occur without warning. Knowing how to protect yourself can reduce the risk of injury.

Drop, Cover, and Hold On

Drop to your hands and knees, protect your head and neck, and take cover under a sturdy table or desk if available. Hold on until the shaking stops.

Avoid Hazards

Stay away from windows, glass, shelves, and objects that may fall. If you are indoors, remain inside until the shaking stops.

Evacuate Safely

After the shaking stops, watch for hazards and evacuate if necessary. Use stairs instead of elevators and proceed to the designated assembly area when it is safe.

FIRE

Fire Safety Guidelines

Know how to respond to a fire and evacuate safely from your home, classroom, or workplace.

Raise the Alarm

Alert people nearby and activate the fire alarm if available. Contact the appropriate emergency service once you are in a safe location.

Evacuate Immediately

Use the nearest safe exit and follow designated evacuation routes. Do not use elevators or return to collect belongings.

Stay Out

Once outside, move away from the building and proceed to the assembly area. Never re-enter a burning or unsafe building until authorities declare it safe.

FLOODS AND TYPHOONS

Flood and Typhoon Safety Guidelines

Severe weather can cause flooding, strong winds, landslides, and dangerous travel conditions. Prepare early and monitor official advisories.

Monitor Advisories

Follow official weather bulletins and local disaster announcements. Prepare essential supplies before conditions worsen.

Avoid Floodwaters

Never walk, swim, or drive through floodwaters. Water may conceal open drains, debris, strong currents, or electrical hazards.

Move to Safety

Follow evacuation instructions from local authorities. Move to a safer location before routes become dangerous or inaccessible.

Your Safety Comes First

These guidelines provide general preparedness information. Always follow instructions from authorized emergency responders and local disaster management authorities.
