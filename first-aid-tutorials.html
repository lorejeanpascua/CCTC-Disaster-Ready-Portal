BASIC FIRST AID

Know What To Do

These tutorials provide basic guidance for common emergencies. Always seek professional medical help when needed.

01

MINOR BLEEDING

Apply gentle pressure to the wound using clean cloth or gauze. Keep pressure on the wound and seek medical attention for serious bleeding.

02

MINOR BURNS

Cool a minor burn with clean, cool running water. Do not apply ice directly to the burn.

03

FAINTING

Keep the person safe from further injury and check whether they are responsive. Seek medical help if they do not recover normally.

04

SPRAINS

Stop the activity and protect the injured area from further movement. Seek medical advice when the injury is severe.

05

CHOKING

If a person cannot breathe, speak, or cough, call emergency services and follow appropriate first-aid instructions for choking.

06

UNRESPONSIVE PERSON

Check for responsiveness and normal breathing. Call emergency services and follow instructions from trained responders.

Keep A First-Aid Kit Ready

A basic first-aid kit can help you respond to minor injuries while waiting for additional help.

  • Adhesive bandages
  • Clean gauze and medical tape
  • Antiseptic supplies
  • Disposable gloves
  • Clean scissors

Know Your Limits

First aid is not a replacement for professional medical treatment.

  • Call emergency services for serious injuries.
  • Follow instructions from medical responders.
  • Do not perform procedures you are not trained to do.

Get Emergency Help

When an emergency is serious, contact emergency responders as soon as possible.

  • Stay calm when giving information.
  • Clearly state your location.
  • Follow the responder's instructions.
FIRST AID REMINDER

Stay Calm And Ask For Help

Basic first aid can help while waiting for professional assistance, but serious injuries require trained medical attention.
