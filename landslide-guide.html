LANDSLIDE SAFETY GUIDE

Stay Alert.
Know What To Do.

Landslides can happen when soil, rocks, and other materials suddenly move down a slope. Heavy rain, earthquakes, and unstable ground can increase the risk. Knowing the warning signs and following safety instructions can help protect you and others.

PREPARE

Three Things To Remember

Knowing the warning signs and having a plan can help you respond quickly during a landslide.

01

WATCH

Watch for warning signs such as cracks, unusual ground movement, falling rocks, or sudden changes in water flow.

02

MOVE

Move away from slopes and landslide-prone areas when authorities give warnings or when danger is present.

03

STAY INFORMED

Listen to official warnings and follow evacuation instructions from authorities and school personnel.

SAFETY TIMELINE

Before, During & After

Follow these important safety steps when there is a risk of landslide.

BEFORE

Know the Warning Signs

  • Learn whether your area is near a landslide-prone slope or location.
  • Watch for cracks in the ground, walls, roads, or other structures.
  • Pay attention to unusual sounds, falling rocks, or moving soil.
  • Know your evacuation routes and designated assembly areas.
  • Prepare an emergency Go Bag and keep important emergency contacts.
DURING

Move Away From Danger

  • Evacuate immediately when instructed by authorities or school personnel.
  • Move away from the path of the landslide and unstable slopes.
  • Stay away from areas where rocks, soil, or debris may continue to fall.
  • Do not attempt to cross an active landslide area.
  • Stay alert for additional landslides and follow official announcements.
AFTER

Stay Away From the Affected Area

  • Do not return to the affected area until authorities say it is safe.
  • Stay away from damaged buildings, unstable slopes, and debris.
  • Watch for additional landslides, especially after heavy rain.
  • Check yourself and others for injuries and request help when needed.
  • Report hazards or damaged areas to responsible personnel or authorities.
CCTC REMINDER

Follow School Evacuation Procedures

If a landslide threatens the school or nearby communities, remain calm and follow the instructions of teachers, school personnel, and emergency responders. Proceed to the designated safe area when instructed.
