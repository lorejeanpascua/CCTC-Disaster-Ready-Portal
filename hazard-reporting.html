HAZARD REPORTING

See A Hazard.
Report It Quickly.

Help keep the CCTC community safe by reporting hazards, unsafe conditions, or damaged facilities that may cause accidents or emergencies.

REPORT A HAZARD

Tell Us What You Found

Provide enough information so the reported hazard can be identified and addressed.

WHAT TO REPORT

Watch For These Hazards

Report conditions that may put students, teachers, staff, or visitors at risk.

01

BLOCKED EXITS

Report doors, hallways, stairways, or emergency exits that are blocked or difficult to access.

02

ELECTRICAL HAZARDS

Report exposed wires, damaged outlets, sparks, or other unsafe electrical conditions.

03

SLIPPERY AREAS

Report wet floors, spills, or other conditions that could cause someone to fall.

04

DAMAGED FACILITIES

Report damaged walls, ceilings, stairs, windows, furniture, or other facilities.

SAFETY REMINDER

Do Not Put Yourself In Danger

If a hazard is dangerous or presents an immediate threat, move away from the area and notify a teacher, school personnel, or emergency responder.
