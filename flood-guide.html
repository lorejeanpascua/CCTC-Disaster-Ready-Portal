FLOOD SAFETY GUIDE

Stay Alert.
Know What To Do.

Flooding can happen quickly during heavy rain and severe weather. Knowing how to prepare and respond can help protect you, your family, and the CCTC community.

DURING A FLOOD

Move. Stay Safe. Stay Informed.

Remember these three important actions when flooding occurs.

1

MOVE

Move to a safer and higher location when instructed to evacuate or when floodwater threatens your area.

2

STAY SAFE

Stay away from floodwater and avoid walking or driving through flooded areas.

3

STAY INFORMED

Listen to official announcements and follow instructions from school personnel and responders.

Before a Flood

Preparation can help you respond safely when flooding occurs.

  • Monitor weather reports and official flood warnings.
  • Know the designated evacuation routes and assembly areas.
  • Prepare an emergency Go Bag and important supplies.
  • Keep important documents and valuables in a safe, protected place.
  • Know how to contact family members and emergency personnel.

During a Flood

Stay calm and follow official instructions.

  • Move to higher ground or a designated safe area when instructed.
  • Avoid walking through moving or unknown floodwater.
  • Never attempt to cross a flooded road or bridge.
  • Stay away from electrical equipment and areas affected by floodwater.
  • Listen to official announcements for evacuation and safety instructions.

After a Flood

Continue to be careful even after the water begins to recede.

  • Wait for official instructions before returning to affected areas.
  • Avoid floodwater because it may contain hazards or contaminants.
  • Stay away from damaged buildings and electrical equipment.
  • Check yourself and others for injuries.
  • Report dangerous conditions to school personnel or emergency responders.
CCTC COMMUNITY REMINDER

Follow Flood Evacuation Procedures

During flooding, always listen to teachers, school personnel, and designated emergency responders. If evacuation is required, proceed calmly to the assigned safe area or assembly point.
