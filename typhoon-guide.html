TYPHOON SAFETY GUIDE

Stay Alert.
Know What To Do.

Typhoons can bring strong winds, heavy rainfall, flooding, and other dangerous conditions. Knowing what to do before, during, and after a typhoon can help keep you and others safe.

PREPARE

Three Things To Remember

Simple actions can help you stay safe during a typhoon.

01

PREPARE

Prepare your Go Bag, food, water, medicines, and other important supplies before the typhoon arrives.

02

SHELTER

Stay in a safe indoor area and keep away from windows, glass, and other areas exposed to strong winds.

03

STAY INFORMED

Listen to official weather updates, warnings, and evacuation instructions from authorities.

SAFETY TIMELINE

Before, During & After

Follow these important safety steps throughout a typhoon.

BEFORE

Prepare Early

  • Monitor official weather advisories and warnings.
  • Prepare a Go Bag with water, food, medicines, flashlight, and other important supplies.
  • Secure loose objects and check doors and windows around your home or school.
  • Know your evacuation routes and designated assembly areas.
  • Charge your phone and other communication devices.
DURING

Stay Safe and Follow Instructions

  • Stay indoors in a safe area away from windows and glass.
  • Follow evacuation orders from school personnel and authorities.
  • Avoid floodwater, flooded roads, and areas with strong water currents.
  • Stay away from fallen or damaged electrical lines.
  • Continue listening to official announcements and weather updates.
AFTER

Stay Cautious

  • Wait for official announcements before leaving a safe area.
  • Avoid damaged buildings, floodwater, and fallen electrical wires.
  • Check yourself and others for possible injuries.
  • Report dangerous conditions or hazards to responsible personnel.
  • Follow school instructions before returning to normal activities.
CCTC REMINDER

Follow School Safety Procedures

During a typhoon or severe weather event, follow the instructions of teachers, school personnel, and emergency responders. Stay calm and proceed to the designated safe or evacuation area when instructed.
