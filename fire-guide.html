FIRE SAFETY GUIDE

Stay Alert.
Know What To Do.

A fire can happen unexpectedly. Knowing what to do before, during, and after a fire can help protect you, your family, and the CCTC community.

DURING A FIRE

Alert. Evacuate. Stay Safe.

Remember these three important actions when a fire occurs.

1

ALERT

Alert people nearby and follow the school's fire emergency procedures.

2

EVACUATE

Leave the building using the designated evacuation route. Stay calm and do not push others.

3

STAY SAFE

Move to the designated assembly area and never return to the building until it is safe.

Before a Fire

Preparation is important before a fire happens.

  • Know the emergency exits and evacuation routes.
  • Identify the designated evacuation and assembly areas.
  • Keep hallways, exits, and doorways clear.
  • Know where fire alarms and fire extinguishers are located.
  • Participate seriously in school fire drills.

During a Fire

Stay calm and focus on getting to a safe location.

  • Stay calm and immediately alert people nearby.
  • Follow the school's evacuation procedures.
  • Leave through the nearest safe exit.
  • Stay low if there is smoke.
  • Do not use elevators during a fire emergency.
  • Never go back inside the building for personal belongings.

After a Fire

Remain in a safe area and follow official instructions.

  • Proceed calmly to the designated assembly area.
  • Stay with your class or group.
  • Inform teachers or emergency responders if someone is missing.
  • Follow instructions from school personnel and emergency responders.
  • Do not return to the building until authorities say it is safe.
CCTC COMMUNITY REMINDER

Take Fire Safety Seriously

During emergencies, always listen to teachers, school personnel, and designated emergency responders. Proceed calmly to the assigned evacuation or assembly area.
