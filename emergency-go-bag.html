GO BAG TIPS

Keep Your Bag Ready

Keep It Easy To Carry

Avoid packing unnecessary items. Your emergency bag should be practical and comfortable enough to carry during evacuation.

Check Supplies Regularly

Check your emergency supplies regularly and replace expired, damaged, or used items.

Keep It Accessible

Store the go bag in a place where family members can easily find and grab it when an emergency requires evacuation.

CCTC Safety Reminder

Prepare your emergency bag before an emergency happens. Know where it is and make sure your family members know what to do during evacuation.
